# Font registry: display faces for the 310 theme ideas

**How this list is maintained:** every theme owns exactly one display face and no two themes share one. A new theme must claim an unused display family in this table first (add its row, then write its `- Type:` line), and a theme that changes its display face must update its row here in the same edit. Body and label faces may repeat across themes, but should not be another theme's display face. Re-run the uniqueness check at the bottom after every change.

Sources are limited to Google Fonts, Fontshare, Velvetyne, Collletttivo and other OFL foundries. Each family was checked against the Google Fonts metadata feed, the Fontshare API or the foundry page on 27 September 2026. Faces banned or flagged in ANTI-VIBE.md (T1, T2) are not used as display faces.
