### XI. Health, wellbeing & beauty

> **Shared ground for 111–124 (from the sites studied):** every practice publishes a fees or prices page, a cancellation clause, and a free first call, screening or consultation. Booking almost always runs on a dedicated clinical or studio system (Cliniko, Jane, IntakeQ, SimplePractice, Mindbody, Momence, Vetstoria, Zenoti), so these themes embed the owner's existing booker, or fall back to **Simply Schedule Appointments** / **Cal.com** for solo practitioners. None of these themes stores health data. Forms only collect contact details plus a "reason for enquiry" select. That rule is written into every theme's README.

#### 111 Therapist / Counsellor (`session`)
**For:** solo counsellors and psychotherapists, and 2–8 person group practices (UKCP/BACP in the UK, LPC/LCSW in the US) who fill their diary through directories and want their own site to do the explaining.
**Templates:** front-page, page-approach, archive-therapist, single-therapist, page-fees, page-first-meetings, page-issues (hub), single-issue, page-faq, page-contact
**Patterns (~40):** fees & cancellation policy block (session length / fee / notice period / why the policy exists), sliding-scale range with "how we set your fee" note, fee-by-clinician-tier table (senior / associate / trainee), "First meetings" explainer (assessment, then matching to a therapist), numbered intake path (call → message → 20-min screening → first session), intake open/closed banner, "Issues we work with" list (anxiety, bereavement, trauma…), therapist profile (modality, registration body + number, languages, days available), professional-body row (UKCP / BACP / BPS, or state licence), out-of-network + superbill note, Good Faith Estimate notice (US), crisis numbers footer.
**Content model:** `therapist` (tier, modalities, registration body, registration no., languages, pronouns, accepting_new: yes/no, fee range), `issue` taxonomy linking therapists to issue pages.
**Plugins:** Jetpack Forms (enquiry form that asks only for a name, contact and preferred time; no clinical details), Query Filter (therapists by issue and language), Simply Schedule Appointments or Cal.com embed for the free screening call.
**Signature:** a practice-wide **intake status banner** plus a numbered "How to get started" path. One site setting (intake open / waitlist / closed) is bound to the banner, to every therapist card, and to the contact form's intro text, so a closed practice stops collecting enquiries it can't answer. (seen on: [Therapy Center of Philadelphia: How to Get Started](https://therapycenterofphila.org/how-to-get-started/))
**Nice-to-haves:**
- "Accepting new clients: yes / waitlist / no" flag on each therapist card, bound to `accepting_new` (operational). (seen on: [Therapy Center of Philadelphia](https://therapycenterofphila.org/how-to-get-started/))
- A sliding-scale explainer with the real range, how the fee is agreed, and a line on "pay what you can" places with trainees (trust & proof). (seen on: [Helping Hands Psychotherapy: Fees](https://www.hhptherapy.com/fees))
- A cancellation policy written in plain English that explains why the notice period exists (care & aftercare). (seen on: [The Koan Practice: Fees & cancellation](https://koan-psy.com/our-fees-cancellation-policies/))
- A persistent footer block with crisis numbers (Samaritans 116 123, NHS 111 option 2, or 988 in the US) and the line "This practice cannot respond to emergencies". A "Leave this site" quick-exit link goes to a neutral page (accessibility & inclusion). None of the practices studied had one, so this is a deliberate addition.
- A printable one-page "fees & policies" sheet: the fees page with print CSS, for clients who want paper (owner tools).
**Style: Quiet clinical-humanist, large-print**
- Type: headings Lustria 400 (tracking left at the face's default); body Atkinson Hyperlegible 400 at 19px / 1.65; small meta Atkinson Hyperlegible 700 at 15px.
- Palette: bg #F7F8F6 (cool paper), text #1F2A2E, accent #2F6B5E (deep eucalyptus, used for links and the single primary button), surface #E4EBE7, line #B9C6C0, crisis box #8E2A24 (used only in the crisis footer).
- Layout: left-aligned single column at 64ch for prose. Fees sit in a real two-column table, not cards. Therapist cards are a list with a portrait on the left and facts on the right. There is no hero headline; the page opens with one sentence on who the practice is for, then the fees link and intake status. Buttons have 4px radius, images 0, inputs 2px. Nav is a plain text row with no hamburger on desktop.
- Imagery: the therapy rooms photographed empty in daylight (chair, window, plant), plus honest head-and-shoulders portraits. No hands-on-knees stock, no brains, no puzzle pieces.
- Motion: none on scroll. Details/accordion open with a 150ms height transition, which is disabled under prefers-reduced-motion. View Transitions between the therapist list and profile.
- Why it fits now: accessibility-first typography (Atkinson Hyperlegible's 2024 "Next" release) has become a visible design choice rather than a compliance afterthought, and a calm practice benefits most from it. Example: [Braille Institute: Atkinson Hyperlegible](https://brailleinstitute.org/freefont).
**Style variations:** Daylight: #F7F8F6 bg, eucalyptus #2F6B5E links, Evening: #1E2624 bg, #D6E2DC text, no glow, Print: black on white, serif headings only.
**Real-world basis:** [Koan: Fees & cancellation](https://koan-psy.com/our-fees-cancellation-policies/), [Koan: First meetings](https://koan-psy.com/first-meetings/), [HHP: Fees & insurance](https://www.hhptherapy.com/fees), [LA Psychotherapy Group: Fees](https://lapsychotherapygroup.org/fees-insurance), [Therapy Center of Philadelphia: How to get started](https://therapycenterofphila.org/how-to-get-started/)
