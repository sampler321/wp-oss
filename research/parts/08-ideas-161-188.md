### XVI. Civic, political & nonprofit

#### 161 Local Election Candidate (`ballot`)
**For:** first-time and independent candidates for council, mayor, school board or a by-election, and small local parties running a slate of ward candidates.
**Templates:** front-page (donate takeover), page-meet-the-candidate, archive-priority, single-priority, page-endorsements, page-record, page-events, page-volunteer, page-how-to-vote, page-privacy
**Patterns (~40):** donate takeover with preset amounts ($10 / $25 / $100 / $250 / other), sign-up strip (name, email, postcode, mobile + SMS consent), priorities grid (one tile per issue linking to its own page), "Meet [name]" biography, endorsements list (people and organisations), accomplishments / record timeline, candidates-by-ward list with postcode search, events list (canvassing, hustings, "Day of Action"), how-to-vote box (registration deadline, postal/proxy vote, polling day), imprint footer ("Promoted by … on behalf of …").
**Content model:** `priority` (short title, one-line pledge, order), `endorsement` (name, role, type: person/org/union), `candidate` (ward, bio, photo) for slates; `event` (date, place, type). Ward taxonomy.
**Plugins:** Jetpack Forms (sign-up, volunteer), GiveWP or Jetpack Donations (or a link out to ActBlue/CrowdPac where law requires), Advanced Query Loop (upcoming events only), Polylang where the ward is multilingual.
**Signature:** a homepage donate takeover with preset-amount buttons that deep-link into the donation form, switched off after polling day (seen on: [Zohran for NYC](https://www.zohranfornyc.com/)).
**Nice-to-haves:**
- Imprint line in the footer ("Promoted by [agent] on behalf of [candidate], both at [address]"), required for UK election material, bound to site settings (seen on: [Hackney Greens: Candidates](https://www.hackneygreens.org.uk/candidates)).
- "Away on polling day? Apply for a postal or proxy vote" box with the deadline date, which hides itself after the deadline (seen on: [Sheffield Green Party: Elections](https://sheffieldgreenparty.org.uk/elections/)).
- Postcode-to-ward lookup that shows "your candidate" on multi-ward slates (seen on: [Hackney Greens: Candidates](https://www.hackneygreens.org.uk/candidates)).
- Accomplishments record for incumbents, as a dated list (seen on: [Alexis for Seattle: Accomplishments](https://www.alexisforseattle.com/accomplishments)).
- Local public-funding explainer where it exists, e.g. Seattle's Democracy Vouchers (seen on: [Alexis for Seattle: Vouchers](https://www.alexisforseattle.com/vouchers)).
- Printable A5 leaflet version of the priorities page for canvassers.
**Style: Campaign poster takeover**
- Type: headings Passion One 900, set huge (12–16vw on the takeover) with normal tracking; body Public Sans 400/18px; small print and imprint IBM Plex Mono 400.
- Palette: bg #FFFFFF, ink #111111, campaign colour #F5C400 (hi-vis yellow, swappable per party), second colour #1D3FBF (ballot-paper blue), line #111111.
- Layout: full-bleed poster block on the front page with one slogan and the donate buttons as fat rectangular slabs (0 radius); below that a strict 4-column priorities grid with black 2px rules. Sticky slim top bar with only "Donate" and "Volunteer". No hero image carousel.
- Imagery: candidate photographed on real local streets in daylight, cut square and printed as a one-colour duotone (ink on the campaign colour); no studio headshots against grey.
- Motion: the takeover collapses to a bar after first dismissal (remembered in localStorage); View Transitions between priority tiles and their pages; nothing animates under prefers-reduced-motion.
- Why it fits now: the 2025 New York mayoral campaign made fly-poster type and one loud colour the grammar of insurgent local campaigns ([zohranfornyc.com](https://www.zohranfornyc.com/)).
**Style variations:** Fly-post: yellow bg, black type, Ballot: white, ballot-blue slabs, Night canvass: #111 bg, yellow type
**Real-world basis:** [Zohran for NYC](https://www.zohranfornyc.com/), [Alexis for Seattle: Priorities](https://www.alexisforseattle.com/priorities), [Hackney Greens: Candidates 2026](https://www.hackneygreens.org.uk/candidates), [Sheffield Green Party: Walkley ward priorities](https://sheffieldgreenparty.org.uk/2026/09/14/water-litter-and-public-libraries-sebs-priorities-for-walkley-ward/), [Run for Something: Local office guide](https://runforsomethingcivics.net/local-guide/)
**Refs:**
- [Zohran for NYC: home](https://www.zohranfornyc.com/): poster-scale slogan, preset donate amounts, SMS sign-up and a treasurer line in the footer; the takeover pattern to copy.
- [Alexis for Seattle: Priorities](https://www.alexisforseattle.com/priorities): eight priorities as a grid, each with its own page; a clean model for `priority` posts.
- [Hackney Greens: Candidates](https://www.hackneygreens.org.uk/candidates): one page per ward with a postcode search; how a small party runs a slate.

#### 162 Local Councillor (`ward`)
**For:** sitting ward and parish councillors (and small council groups) who report back to residents between elections.
**Templates:** front-page, archive (ward reports), single, taxonomy-issue, page-about-me, page-surgeries, page-report-a-problem, page-useful-links, page-contact
**Patterns (~35):** monthly ward-report card (month, headline issues, tags), issue tags (fly-tipping, rats, ASB, greening, licensing, planning), councillor profile (ward, committee roles, council email/phone), "Report it" link panel (council service, FixMyStreet, WriteToThem), surgery dates list (date / venue / time), casework-update post, "introducing my ward colleague" card, useful-websites directory, planning and licensing application notices, imprint line.
**Content model:** posts (ward reports) with an `issue` taxonomy; `surgery` (date, venue, start/end, booking needed y/n); `councillor` (ward, party, committees, contact) for group sites.
**Plugins:** Jetpack Forms (casework contact with consent tick), Advanced Query Loop (next surgery only), Polylang where needed.
**Signature:** a monthly ward-report series with every post tagged by local issue, so residents can open "everything on fly-tipping" (seen on: [Forest Gate North: Ward report](https://forestgatenorth.com/2020/09/24/ward-report-september-2020/)).
**Nice-to-haves:**
- "Useful websites" page that points residents to the right council service before they email (seen on: [Forest Gate North: Useful websites](https://forestgatenorth.com/useful-websites/)).
- Report-it panel with deep links to FixMyStreet and the council's own forms (seen on: [FixMyStreet](https://www.fixmystreet.com/)).
- Committee roles and council contact details on the profile, bound from `councillor` meta (seen on: [Bristol Green Party: Cllr Ed Plowden](https://bristolgreenparty.org.uk/councillor/ed-plowden/)).
- "Promoted by and on behalf of" line, plus an automatic pre-election-period notice that pauses campaign posts during purdah (seen on: [Bristol Green Party: Cllr Ed Plowden](https://bristolgreenparty.org.uk/councillor/ed-plowden/)).
- Casework form with a clear "What I can and can't help with" note and a response-time promise.
- Printable surgery poster (A4) for the library noticeboard, generated from the next `surgery` post.
**Style: Civic utility (public-service design lineage)**
- Type: headings B612 700 at large sizes (drawn for cockpit-screen legibility); body Atkinson Hyperlegible Next 400/19px; dates and ward names Newsreader 500 italic for a second, human voice.
- Palette: bg #FFFFFF, text #0B0C0C, ward green #00703C, focus/highlight #FFDD00, line #B1B4B6, surface #F3F2F1.
- Layout: single 66ch reading column with a left index of issues; wide black 5px top border on the header like a council notice; "Report it" as full-width link rows, not cards. Mobile-first; no carousels.
- Imagery: real photos of the ward (bins, parks, the estate), taken by the councillor, shown at 3:2 with captions and dates; no stock handshake images.
- Motion: none beyond focus states and underline thickening on hover; View Transitions between report archive and single.
- Why it fits now: plain public-service design is the trusted register for civic information in 2025–26 and is what residents already know from [GOV.UK](https://www.gov.uk/).
**Style variations:** Town hall: green rule, white page, Parish: warm #F3F2F1 surface, serif dates, Night: #0B0C0C bg, yellow focus
**Real-world basis:** [Forest Gate North: Ward report](https://forestgatenorth.com/2020/09/24/ward-report-september-2020/), [Forest Gate North: Betting shop licence post](https://forestgatenorth.com/2025/07/31/betting-shop-licence-application/), [Bristol Green Party: Cllr Ed Plowden](https://bristolgreenparty.org.uk/councillor/ed-plowden/), [WriteToThem](https://www.writetothem.com/), [TheyWorkForYou](https://www.theyworkforyou.com/)
**Refs:**
- [Forest Gate North: Ward report](https://forestgatenorth.com/2020/09/24/ward-report-september-2020/): a councillor's own WordPress blog with monthly reports tagged by issue; the core content habit.
- [Bristol Green Party: Cllr Ed Plowden](https://bristolgreenparty.org.uk/councillor/ed-plowden/): councillor profile built from a custom post type (ward, role, contacts, latest news); the `councillor` model.
- [WriteToThem](https://www.writetothem.com/): postcode-first civic utility; the plain, trustworthy register to aim for.

#### 163 Tenants' Union (`tenants`)

**Content model:** `right` (topic, jurisdiction, last reviewed date, summary), `campaign` (status: live/won/closed), `branch` (area, meeting night, venue), `event`. Language taxonomy via Polylang.
**Plugins:** Jetpack Forms (join, member defence), GiveWP (donations and dues via recurring), The Events Calendar or companion `event` + Advanced Query Loop, Polylang.
**Signature:** a join page where dues are set by your hourly wage, with fixed special rates for unemployed and retired members (seen on: [ACORN: Join](https://acorntheunion.org.uk/join/)).
**Nice-to-haves:**
- Walk-in clinic hours and hotline times at the top of the rights pages ("Tuesdays, arrive by 6:30pm") (seen on: [Met Council on Housing: Walk-in clinic](https://www.metcouncilonhousing.org/tenants--rights-mutual-walk-in-clinic)).
- "Up your dues" page for existing members after a pay rise (seen on: [ACORN: Update your dues](https://acorntheunion.org.uk/join/update-your-membership-dues/)).
- Member-defence request form for bailiffs and illegal evictions, with an "if it's happening now, call…" line (seen on: [ACORN: Member defence](https://acorntheunion.org.uk/contact/bailiff-member-defence/)).
- "Last reviewed" date and a "this is not legal advice" note on every rights page, bound from `right` meta.
- Translated rights pages, with the language list shown as words not flags.
- Union elections and AGM notices with nomination deadlines (seen on: [London Renters Union: Elections 2026](https://londonrentersunion.org/2026/lru-elections-2026/)).
**Style: Protest-poster black and white**
- Type: headings Protest Strike 400 in capitals, set tight but not crushed (0 tracking), used at poster scale; body Public Sans 400/18px; labels Public Sans 600.
- Palette: bg #FFFFFF, ink #000000, action red #D7261E, newsprint grey #E9E9E4 (surface), line #000000.
- Layout: two-column poster grid; the demands list as numbered full-width bands; rights pages as a long single column with a sticky topic index. Buttons are hard black rectangles; forms have 2px black borders.
- Imagery: black-and-white photos of real actions and door-knocking, high contrast, cropped tight on placards and faces with consent; hand-lettered placards used as found type.
- Motion: none on content; red underline swipe on link hover (transform only); fully static under prefers-reduced-motion.
- Why it fits now: renters' unions have grown fast since 2020 and the black-and-white poster look signals "movement, not charity" ([London Renters Union](https://londonrentersunion.org/)).
**Style variations:** Placard: red bg, black type, Newsprint: grey paper, black ink, Nightshift: black bg, white type
**Real-world basis:** [ACORN: Join](https://acorntheunion.org.uk/join/), [ACORN: Renters' rights](https://acorntheunion.org.uk/renters-rights/), [London Renters Union: Calendar](https://londonrentersunion.org/calendar/), [London Renters Union: Demands](https://londonrentersunion.org/demands/), [Met Council on Housing: Walk-in clinic](https://www.metcouncilonhousing.org/tenants--rights-mutual-walk-in-clinic)
**Refs:**
- [ACORN: Join](https://acorntheunion.org.uk/join/): wage-linked dues with special rates; the fairest join flow in the sector.
- [ACORN: Renters' rights](https://acorntheunion.org.uk/renters-rights/): rights split by topic in plain language; the template for `right` posts.
- [London Renters Union: Demands](https://londonrentersunion.org/demands/): short, numbered demands in poster type; the visual register for this theme.

#### 164 Trade Union Branch (`branch`)
**For:** workplace and local union branches (university UCU branches, IWW and independent unions, hospital and council branches) run by volunteer officers.

**Signature:** a ballot information page with voting dates, the 50% turnout rule, replacement-ballot instructions and an FAQ, surfaced site-wide while the ballot is open (seen on: [Goldsmiths UCU: Upcoming ballot](https://goldsmithsucu.org/2026/03/10/information-on-gucu-upcoming-ballot-march-16-to-april-10-2026/)).

- Translated know-your-rights pages for migrant workers (seen on: [UVW: Know your rights](https://www.uvwunion.org.uk/en/know-your-rights)).
- "Update your details" form for members who change job or address (seen on: [Cambridge UCU: Update your details](https://www.ucu.cam.ac.uk/contact/update-your-details/)).
- Searchable motions and minutes archive, so a branch's decisions are on the record.
**Style: Banner heritage meets plain bulletin**
- Type: headings Vidaloka 400 (a fat high-contrast serif that recalls painted union banners) at 4–6× body size; body IBM Plex Sans 400/18px; tables, dates and motion numbers IBM Plex Mono 500.
- Palette: bg #FFFFFF, text #1A1A1A, banner crimson #9E1B32, banner gold #C9A227, deep navy #14213D (header band), line #D8D4CC.
- Layout: navy header band with the branch name set like a banner; content below in a plain two-column bulletin (main + "Current ballot / Next meeting" sidebar). Tables use full rules, no zebra striping. Motions read like minutes, numbered.
- Imagery: photos of the branch's own banner, picket lines and meetings; archive scans of old leaflets; no stock handshakes.
- Motion: none; the only state change is the ballot box switching from "open" to "result" by date.

**Style variations:** Banner: navy band, gold serif, Bulletin: white, black tables, Picket: crimson bg, white type
**Real-world basis:** [Goldsmiths UCU: Departmental reps](https://goldsmithsucu.org/departmental-reps/), [Goldsmiths UCU: Ballot info](https://goldsmithsucu.org/2026/03/10/information-on-gucu-upcoming-ballot-march-16-to-april-10-2026/), [Cambridge UCU: Join](https://www.ucu.cam.ac.uk/join/), [IWW London branch](https://iww.org.uk/london/), [UVW: Know your rights](https://www.uvwunion.org.uk/en/know-your-rights)

#### 165 Mutual Aid Network (`mutual`)
**For:** neighbourhood mutual aid groups, solidarity networks and free stores run by volunteers with little money and less time.
**Templates:** front-page (two doors: need / offer), page-request-help, page-offer-help, page-donate-items, page-calendar, archive-resource, single-resource, page-faq, page-about
**Patterns (~30):** two-door front page ("I need help" / "I can help"), request form with a phone-line alternative, offer / volunteer form with skills checkboxes, items wishlist ("We need: nappies size 4, tinned fish"), capacity notice ("Deliveries currently take 3–5 days"), confidentiality statement, anonymised requests map by postcode area, calendar (free store, distribution days), resource library by topic (food, bail funds, childcare), seasonal projects (heatwave buddies, fruit gleaning), FAQ.
**Content model:** `resource` (topic, link, last checked), `need` (item, quantity, urgency), `event`. No personal data stored in posts.
**Plugins:** Jetpack Forms (request, offer), Out of the Block: OpenStreetMap (anonymised map), Polylang, Jetpack Donations.
**Signature:** separate "Request help" and "Offer help" pathways from the front page, each a short form, with a phone number for people without internet (seen on: [Berkeley Mutual Aid: Need assistance](https://www.berkeleymutualaid.org/need-assistance) and [Offering assistance](https://www.berkeleymutualaid.org/offering-assistance)).
**Nice-to-haves:**
- Capacity notice set by an admin toggle, e.g. "Deliveries currently take 3–5 days" (seen on: [ROC Mutual Aid: Request aid](https://rocmutualaid.com/requests)).
- Anonymised fulfilled-requests map by postcode area, which proves the work without exposing anyone (seen on: [ROC Mutual Aid: Requests map](https://rocmutualaid.com/resources/requests-map)).
- Items wishlist that volunteers can update without editing the page (seen on: [ROC Mutual Aid: Donate items](https://rocmutualaid.com/donate/items)).
- Seasonal buddy schemes switched on by date (heatwave, wildfire smoke, cold snap) (seen on: [Berkeley Mutual Aid: Wildfire buddies](https://www.berkeleymutualaid.org/wildfire-buddies)).
- Printable A4 "we're here" poster with a tear-off phone number strip, for lamp posts and launderettes.
- Plain-language and translated versions of the request page.
**Style: Web 1.0 text-first revival**
- Type: headings Mukta 800 at large sizes, normal tracking; body Atkinson Hyperlegible 400/19px; links underlined always.
- Palette: bg #FFFFFF, text #111111, link blue #0033CC, visited #333399, highlight #FFF2A8 (marker yellow), line #111111.
- Layout: one column, 70ch, no cards; the two doors are two huge text links stacked on mobile and side by side on desktop. Lists everywhere. Page weight under 200 KB so it loads on a prepaid phone.
- Imagery: almost none; a hand-drawn map or photocopied flyer scans at most. Photos only of real distribution days, with consent.
- Motion: none. Pages must work with JS off.
- Why it fits now: low-bandwidth, text-first sites are back in favour with organisers and link-sharing communities, as on [Are.na](https://www.are.na/), and they load for the people who most need them.
**Style variations:** Photocopy: grey #EDEDED paper, black type, Marker: yellow highlight headings, Night line: black bg, white type
**Real-world basis:** [ROC Mutual Aid: Request aid](https://rocmutualaid.com/requests), [ROC Mutual Aid: Requests map](https://rocmutualaid.com/resources/requests-map), [Berkeley Mutual Aid: Need assistance](https://www.berkeleymutualaid.org/need-assistance), [MANA: Free store offer](https://www.wearemana.org/free-store-offer-services-or-goods), [Big Door Brigade: Mutual aid toolbox](https://bigdoorbrigade.com/mutual-aid-toolbox/)
**Refs:**
- [ROC Mutual Aid: Request aid](https://rocmutualaid.com/requests): honest capacity notice, confidentiality note and a bilingual phone line on the request page.
- [Berkeley Mutual Aid: Offering assistance](https://www.berkeleymutualaid.org/offering-assistance): the "offer" door; short and specific about what help is needed.
- [Big Door Brigade: Mutual aid toolbox](https://bigdoorbrigade.com/mutual-aid-toolbox/): a WordPress, text-first resource library by topic; the model for `resource`.
