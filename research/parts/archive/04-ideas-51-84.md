
---

> **Note on scope:** ideas 01–50 cover creative fields. From 51 onward the list moves deliberately toward everyday independent sites: shops, food, trades, health, education, tech, civic groups, sport, and personal sites. The brutalist flare stays and gets applied to ordinary things: a plumber's site in Swiss grid and a bakery in riso colours. That mix is how the library stands apart.

### VII. Writing, knowledge & local media

#### 51 · Novelist / Author (`spine`)
**For:** authors with 1–10 books.
**Templates:** front-page · archive-book · single-book · page-events · page-about · page-press · page-newsletter
**Patterns (~40):** new-book hero with retailer buttons (Bookshop.org / indie / Amazon), book card with cover 3D tilt, praise quotes, excerpt/read-a-chapter, events list, book-club guide download, rights/agent contact, newsletter.
**Content model:** `book` (series, pub date, publisher, ISBN, retailer links).
**Plugins:** Jetpack Newsletter, Advanced Query Loop.
**Signature:** bound "Buy from…" button groups driven by ISBN, with indie retailers listed first.
**Flare:** Penguin-grid covers, a colophon on every page.
**Style variations:** Paperback · Hardback · Pulp.
**Refs:** {{REFS-51}}

#### 52 · Independent Journalist / Paid Newsletter (`dispatch`)
**For:** writers leaving Substack who want their own site.
**Templates:** front-page · archive (posts) · single (with paywall) · page-subscribe · page-about · page-archive-by-year
**Patterns (~35):** subscribe hero, free vs paid tiers, paywall divider, "support independent journalism" block, byline card, corrections note, sources/footnotes, related reading.
**Plugins:** Jetpack Newsletter + Paid Content (or MailPoet).
**Signature:** emails and web posts from the same blocks, a "Premium content" block inline, and archive-by-year.
**Flare:** telegram/wire-service styling with typed datelines.
**Style variations:** Broadsheet · Wire · Samizdat.
**Refs:** {{REFS-52}}

#### 53 · Hyperlocal News Site (`local`)
**For:** one-town or one-neighbourhood newsrooms and volunteer community papers.
**Templates:** front-page (news grid) · category · single · archive-author · page-tips · page-donate · page-events · page-about-our-funding
**Patterns (~55):** lead story + river, category sections, breaking-news bar, local events list, "send us a tip" form, donation CTA, obituaries list, council meeting tracker, corrections policy, transparency/funding page.
**Content model:** posts + categories. `event`. Author profiles.
**Plugins:** Jetpack Forms, GiveWP or Jetpack Donations, Advanced Query Loop. (Reference: Automattic's Newspack.)
**Signature:** a council-meeting tracker pattern (date / agenda / outcome) and a donation-first model.
**Flare:** tabloid layouts in strict grids, headline type at 12vw.
**Style variations:** Gazette · Tabloid · Civic.
**Refs:** {{REFS-53}}

#### 58 · Recipe Site / Cookbook Author (`pantry`)
**For:** home cooks, cookbook writers, food bloggers who hate the usual recipe clutter.
**Templates:** front-page · single-recipe · archive-recipe · taxonomy-cuisine · page-cookbook · page-about
**Patterns (~40):** jump-to-recipe, recipe card (ingredients with servings scaler, steps, timings), step photos, substitution notes, cookbook promo, seasonal collection, print view.

**Flare:** index-card recipes, typewriter measurements.
**Style variations:** Index Card · Diner · Test Kitchen.
**Refs:** {{REFS-58}}
