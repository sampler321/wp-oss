### XXVIII. Spaces, hire & unusual services

> **Rental stack (applies to 253–256, and to hireable rooms and kit elsewhere in this batch).** Checked on wordpress.org in September 2026: **Booking and Rental Manager for WooCommerce** (MagePeople, sold as "WpRently", free tier, updated September 2026, 1,000+ installs) handles single-day and multi-day hire with hourly, daily and weekly pricing, per-item inventory, availability-aware date pickers, extra services and add-ons, and WooCommerce block checkout. It ships five blocks: Rental Items List, Search Form, Filter, Add to Cart and Search Results. Its "Security Deposit Management" and admin booking calendar are **Pro-only**, so the themes do this instead. The **refundable deposit** is a required extra-service line on each item, refunded through the normal Woo refund screen. The **damage waiver** is an optional add-on priced as a percentage of the hire. The public availability view is a companion Interactivity-API block that reads booked dates from Woo orders. Rejected: Easy Booking (free, but per-date stock is a paid add-on), WPS Bookings (slot-based, suits appointments rather than kit), YITH Request a Quote (free tier works but is rated 3.3/5 with slow support). Where hire is quote-led (marquees, entertainers, bands), the themes use a **request-to-book** flow: a companion "quote list" block that pre-fills a Jetpack Form with items, date, venue and postcode, as real hire firms do.
