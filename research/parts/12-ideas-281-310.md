#### 281 · Literary Magazine (`lit`)
**For:** small print-and-web literary magazines (two to six editors, two issues a year) that publish fiction, poetry, essays and reviews, run open submission windows and live on subscriptions and stockists.
**Templates:** front-page (current issue) · archive-issue · single-issue (table of contents) · single (piece) · taxonomy-section (Fiction / Poetry / Essays / Reviews) · page-submissions · page-subscribe · page-stockists · page-about · 404
**Patterns (~45):** current-issue cover block with price and "buy this issue", issue table of contents grouped by section with author and first line, piece header (section, issue number, author, translator), contributor note, "from the print issue" / "web only" label, submissions window block (open or closed, categories, fees, payment rates per page), stockist list by country and city, library subscription note, gift subscription, back-issues grid, prize announcement, podcast episode row, newsletter sign-up.
**Content model:** `issue` CPT (number, season, year, price, cover, buy URL, in-print flag) with pieces as posts linked by `issue_id` meta; `section` taxonomy; `contributor` taxonomy with bio term meta; `stockist` CPT (name, city, country, URL). All bound to blocks with Block Bindings.
**Plugins:** WooCommerce (single issues, back issues), Subscriptions for WooCommerce, Jetpack Newsletter.
**Signature:** the issue page as a real table of contents: pieces grouped by section, each with author, opening line and a link to its own page, so an issue reads like the printed contents spread and every piece still has a permanent URL (seen on: [n+1: Issue 54](https://www.nplusonemag.com/issue-54/)). Built with a Query Loop filtered by `issue_id` and grouped by `section`.
**Nice-to-haves:**
- Submissions state block with open and close dates that flips its own copy from "Open until 31 May" to "Closed. Next window announced here", plus payment rates in a plain table (seen on: [The Stinging Fly: Submissions](https://stingingfly.org/submissions/))
- Stockist list grouped by country and state, printable for bookshop buyers (seen on: [n+1: Bookstores](https://www.nplusonemag.com/bookstores/))
- Library and institutional subscription page with invoice-friendly wording (seen on: [n+1: Libraries](https://www.nplusonemag.com/libraries/))
- Gift subscription product with a "start from next issue / current issue" choice and a gift note field
- Article schema.org on every piece and an OG image pattern that sets the issue cover and piece title in the magazine's type

**Style: Editorial serif revival**
- Type: headings Ibarra Real Nova 600, set large with normal tracking; body Ibarra Real Nova 400/19px, 1.55 line-height, 68ch measure; labels and section names Figtree 600 at 14px, sentence case.
- Palette: bg #F7F6F2 (uncoated paper white, cool) / text #1B1B1B / accent #9E1B32 (issue-number red, used only for section names and links) / surface #ECEAE3 / line #C9C5BA.
- Layout: 12-column grid with a narrow left margin column for section names and footnote numbers; pieces sit on columns 4–10. Home is the current issue: cover image left at 5 columns, the full contents list right. Nav is a single text row: Current issue · Archive · Submit · Subscribe. Hairline rules separate sections; corners stay square and content sits directly on the page.
- Imagery: covers shown flat at 3:4, no mock-up tilt; artist pages from the issue shown full-bleed with a caption line underneath, never cropped.
- Motion: none on scroll. Links underline on hover with a 120ms colour change; the contents list uses a View Transition when moving from issue to piece so the title lands in place. Reduced motion removes the transition.
- Why it fits now: independent magazines are moving back to reading-first sites with real contents pages after years of feed layouts, as on [The White Review](https://www.thewhitereview.org/).
**Style variations:** Winter issue: #EEF0F2 bg, slate-blue #2F4A6B accent · Proof: #FFFFFF bg, black text, red corrections · Night reading: #16181B bg, #E9E4D8 text.
**Real-world basis:** [n+1: Issue 54](https://www.nplusonemag.com/issue-54/), [The White Review: Issue No. 33](https://www.thewhitereview.org/issue/issue-no-33/), [The Stinging Fly: Submissions](https://stingingfly.org/submissions/), [n+1: Bookstores](https://www.nplusonemag.com/bookstores/), [n+1: Libraries](https://www.nplusonemag.com/libraries/)
**Refs:**
- [n+1: Issue 54](https://www.nplusonemag.com/issue-54/): contents grouped by section with author and opening lines; the model for `single-issue`.
- [The Stinging Fly: Submissions](https://stingingfly.org/submissions/): an honest closed-window notice, category rules and per-page payment rates.
- [The White Review: Issue No. 33](https://www.thewhitereview.org/issue/issue-no-33/): issue page that lists fiction, poetry, essays and artists with price and buy link.

#### 282 · Poet (`verse`)
**For:** working poets with one to five collections who give readings, teach, and need a place for poems published elsewhere, their books and their dates.
**Templates:** front-page · page-poems (published online) · archive-book · single-book · page-readings (upcoming and past) · page-prose · page-about · page-contact
**Patterns (~30):** selected poems list (title, magazine, year, external link), book entry (cover, publisher, year, prize shortlistings, press quotes, buy links), readings list (date, venue, city, "sold out", ticket link), past readings archive, prose and essays list, bio in short and long versions, contact split by purpose (readings, press, rights), audio track or recorded reading embed, newsletter.
**Content model:** `book` CPT (publisher, year, ISBN, prizes, buy links); `appearance` CPT (date, venue, city, ticket URL, status); "published elsewhere" list as a `link` post format with `venue` and `year` meta.
**Plugins:** Advanced Query Loop (upcoming vs past readings).
**Signature:** a "Poems" page that is a plain list of poems published online, each linking out to the magazine or newspaper where it first appeared, so the site sends readers to the poem's home instead of copying it (seen on: [Jericho Brown](https://www.jerichobrown.com/) and [Ross Gay: Writing](https://www.rossgay.net/writing)).
**Nice-to-haves:**
- "Sold out" and "On sale 3 March" states on readings, with past dates moving to an archive page on their own (seen on: [Hollie McNish: Live dates](https://www.holliepoetry.com/livedates))
- Separate emails for readings, media and general post so bookers reach the right person (seen on: [Jericho Brown](https://www.jerichobrown.com/))
- Book page with prize shortlistings and two press quotes above the buy link (seen on: [Raymond Antrobus: Signs, Music](https://raymondantrobus.com/portfolio/signs-music/))
- Downloadable bio (50 words and 150 words) and author photo with credit, for festival programmes
- Line-break-safe poem template: preformatted whitespace preserved, no justified text, hanging indents kept on mobile

**Style: Web 1.0 revival (plain HTML poetry)**
- Type: everything in Radley 400/19px with italic for titles; headings are the same face at 28px, no second family except IBM Plex Mono 400/14px for dates.
- Palette: bg #FFFFFF / text #111111 / link #0000CC (unvisited) / visited #551A8B / rule #999999.
- Layout: one column, 36em wide, left-aligned, no header image. Nav is a line of text links at the top separated by middle dots. Home is the poet's name, one sentence, the next three readings and the newest book. Default-looking `<hr>`s, styled deliberately thin.
- Imagery: book covers at 180px wide, inline with text; one black-and-white author photo on the About page only.
- Motion: none. Visited-link colours are part of the design. Nothing to reduce.
- Why it fits now: poets and net artists are returning to hand-made HTML-looking pages that load instantly and last, as on [Olia Lialina's art.teleportacia.org](https://art.teleportacia.org/).
**Style variations:** Typescript: IBM Plex Mono throughout, #FAFAF7 bg · Chapbook: #F2EEE6 bg, oxblood #6B1E1E links · Blackout: #000000 bg, #F0F0F0 text.
**Real-world basis:** [Jericho Brown](https://www.jerichobrown.com/), [Ross Gay: Writing](https://www.rossgay.net/writing), [Ada Limón: Calendar](https://adalimon.com/calendar), [Hollie McNish: Live dates](https://www.holliepoetry.com/livedates), [Raymond Antrobus: Signs, Music](https://raymondantrobus.com/portfolio/signs-music/)
**Refs:**
- [Ross Gay: Writing](https://www.rossgay.net/writing): selected poems and essays as outbound links to where they were published, plus audio tracks.
- [Hollie McNish: Live dates](https://www.holliepoetry.com/livedates): a long readings list with clear SOLD OUT flags and "on sale" dates.
- [Ada Limón: Calendar](https://adalimon.com/calendar): short upcoming list with a separate past-events page.
