#### 189 · Walking Tour Guide (`guide`)
**For:** a solo Blue Badge or city guide, or a small collective of 3–10 guides, running scheduled public walks and private tours in one city.
**Templates:** front-page · archive-walk · single-walk · page-walks-by-date · archive-guide · single-guide · page-private-tours · page-gift-vouchers · page-faq · page-contact
**Patterns (~40):** "walks by date" day schedule (time / walk / meeting point / guide), walk detail header (duration, price by adult / student / child / concession, days and start time), meeting-point block ("Tower Hill Tube, by the coffee stand") with map, "just turn up or book ahead" note, guide profile card (Blue Badge / City of London Guide credentials, languages), walks by era (Roman / Medieval / Tudor / Victorian) and by area, private and group tour enquiry form, gift voucher block, cancellation and accessibility notes, press quote strip, walk podcast episode list.
**Content model:** `walk` (duration, meeting_point, meeting_lat/lng, price tiers, weekdays, start_time, booking_url), `departure` (walk, date, time, guide, spaces) linked to a walk, `guide` (credentials, languages, bio), taxonomies `era` and `area`.
**Plugins:** Advanced Query Loop (upcoming departures by date), Out of the Block: OpenStreetMap (meeting-point pin), Jetpack Forms (private tour enquiry), optional FareHarbor or Cal.com embed for paid booking.
**Signature:** a "Walks by date" page that lists each day's departures in time order with walk title, meeting point (down to the station exit) and the named guide linked to their profile, driven by `departure` posts and Advanced Query Loop (seen on: [London Walks](https://www.walks.com/our-walks/walks-by-date/)).
**Nice-to-haves:**
- "No need to book, just turn up" line on walks that allow it, next to the online booking link (seen on: [London Walks: Jack the Ripper](https://www.walks.com/our-walks/jack-the-ripper-walking-tour/)).
- Non-running dates on the walk page ("Not on 24–25 December") that hide those departures automatically (seen on: [London Walks: Jack the Ripper](https://www.walks.com/our-walks/jack-the-ripper-walking-tour/)).
- Gift vouchers for any amount, emailed to the recipient or printed for a card, valid 24 months, with a redeem page (seen on: [London Guided Walks: gift voucher](https://londonguidedwalks.co.uk/gift-voucher/)).
- Accessibility paragraph per walk (terrain, flat or stepped, pace, comfort breaks) plus a plain 48-hour cancellation rule (seen on: [Oxford Official Walking Tours: ghost tour](https://www.oxfordofficialwalkingtours.org/tours/oxford-ghost-tour/)).
- schema.org `TouristTrip` and `Event` markup on each departure so dates show in search.
**Style: Editorial serif revival (pocket guidebook)**
- Type: Headings: Martel 600 upright, 76–96px, line-height 1.0; body: Martel 400/19px, 68ch measure; times and meeting points: IBM Plex Mono 500 16px, normal case.
- Palette: bg #ECEEE8 (pale sage guidebook paper) / text #1B1A17 / accent #DC241F (Underground-map red, used only for times and "meet at") / surface #DDE1D8 / line #1B1A17.
- Layout: two-column book spread on desktop, left column a fixed "departure board" of times, right column long text; hairline rules between entries instead of cards. Radii: 0 on images and lists, 2px on buttons and inputs. Home page opens on today's walks, not a hero image. Top nav: Walks / By date / Guides / Private tours.
- Imagery: guides photographed on location mid-talk, 4:5 portrait crops, lightly desaturated; archival engravings and old street maps as full-bleed section breaks.
- Motion: View Transitions between the date list and a walk page (title carries across); horizontal date strip with scroll-snap; everything static under prefers-reduced-motion.
- Why it fits now: small publishers and guidebook makers have moved back to bookish, serif-led layouts that read as local and trustworthy, e.g. [Hoxton Mini Press](https://www.hoxtonminipress.com/).
**Style variations:** Night walk: #121212 bg, sodium #F2A33A times · Blue Plaque: #1F3F7A panels, white serif · Tube Map: white bg, line colours per era
**Real-world basis:** [London Walks: Jack the Ripper walk](https://www.walks.com/our-walks/jack-the-ripper-walking-tour/), [London Walks: guides](https://www.walks.com/guides/), [London Guided Walks: calendar](https://londonguidedwalks.co.uk/calendar/), [Oxford Official Walking Tours: ghost tour](https://www.oxfordofficialwalkingtours.org/tours/oxford-ghost-tour/)
**Refs:**
- [London Walks: walks by date](https://www.walks.com/our-walks/walks-by-date/): the daily schedule with time, meeting point exit and linked guide name; the core of the theme.
- [London Walks: our guides](https://www.walks.com/guides/): character-driven guide bios with credentials (Blue Badge, City guide) up front.
- [Oxford Official Walking Tours: Oxford Ghost Tour](https://www.oxfordofficialwalkingtours.org/tours/oxford-ghost-tour/): a clear walk page: exact meeting point, price tiers, duration, accessibility, cancellation, booking widget.
