## Part 4: The 310 theme ideas

**How to read each entry**
- **For:** who exactly would use the theme.
- **Templates:** block templates in `templates/`, taken from the page lists of the real sites studied. WooCommerce templates are included when there is a shop.
- **Patterns:** the pre-made block library for that theme, using the words real sites of that type use.
- **Content model:** content types, taxonomies and fields from the `wp-oss-companion` plugin, shown through Block Bindings.
- **Plugins:** only what is essential, from the toolkit in Part 2 unless noted and checked.
- **Signature:** the one feature that makes the theme worth choosing, with a link to the real site where it already works ("seen on").
- **Nice-to-haves:** small, practical touches across buying confidence, trust, conversion, owner tools, local information, seasonal operations, aftercare, community, accessibility and owner quality of life. Recurring ones become shared blocks (Part 5).
- **Real-world basis:** the 3–5 live sites studied to write the entry.
- **Style:** a named direction with exact free fonts (unique display face per theme, see `FONT-REGISTRY.md`), a hex palette, layout, imagery, motion, and a live example of why it is current.
- **Style variations:** three `styles/*.json` variations.
- **Refs:** the 2–3 best deep links to learn from.
- **Screens:** desktop and mobile captures of each Ref, stored in `screenshots/` (click to enlarge). Pages behind bot checks are left out.

Ideas 001–084 were first drafted from memory, then rewritten from scratch against real sites. Every feature that could not be found on a live site was replaced.
