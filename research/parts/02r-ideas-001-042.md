### I. Visual art

#### 001 Illustrator: Portfolio + Shop (`ink`)
**For:** freelance illustrators who sell prints, publications and the odd original, and take private commissions alongside editorial work.
**Templates:** front-page, archive-work (portfolio), single-work, taxonomy-work-category (editorial / posters / packaging / books), page-art-for-sale (originals), page-commissions, page-about, page-contact, Woo: archive-product (shop with Prints / Publications categories), single-product, cart, checkout, order-confirmation, 404
**Patterns (~45):** portfolio grid with category links (Editorial, Posters, Packaging, Product, Exhibitions), work caption (client, publication, year), originals-for-sale list (title, medium such as "ballpen and correction fluid", drawing size, paper size, price or RESERVED), "how to buy an original" steps (email title + postal address, pay by request, arrives within 21 days), shop category row (Prints, Publications), product card with "Sold out" label, commission options table (digital print vs painted, size and price range), commission process list (form, contract + 40% deposit, sketch and colour study, final, balance), commission FAQ (sizing, reference photos, copyright), books list with publisher and retailer links, newsletter block, contact with agent line.
**Content model:** `work` CPT (client, publication, year, `print_product_id`); taxonomy `work_category`. Originals are Woo simple products with stock 1 and a "Reserved" status tag.
**Plugins:** WooCommerce, Jetpack Forms.
**Signature:** an "Art for sale" page that lists each original drawing with medium, drawing and paper size, one flat price and a RESERVED state, and explains in three lines how to buy and when it arrives (seen on: [Tom Gauld: Art for Sale](https://www.tomgauld.com/art-for-sale)).
**Nice-to-haves:**
- Operational/seasonal: "Order by 15 Dec for Christmas delivery" banner with UK / EU / rest-of-world cut-off dates that switches itself off after the last date.
- Buying confidence: print shown at scale beside an A4 sheet, plus a "ships rolled in a tube / flat in board" packing note on every print.
- Conversion helper: "Commissions open / closed" state that swaps the commission form for a waitlist line (process and 40% deposit terms seen on: [Ellen Surrey: Commissions](https://www.ellensurrey.com/commissions)).
- Local & practical: postage-included note by region and "arrives within 21 days" delivery promise (seen on: [Tom Gauld: Art for Sale](https://www.tomgauld.com/art-for-sale)).
- Owner-side quality of life: ready-made commission T&Cs page covering copyright and reproduction rights.
**Real-world basis:** [Tom Gauld: Art for Sale](https://www.tomgauld.com/art-for-sale), [Tom Gauld: Shop](https://www.tomgauld.com/shop), [Jean Jullien: Works](https://www.jeanjullien.com/works/), [Ellen Surrey: Commissions](https://www.ellensurrey.com/commissions)
**Style: Sketchbook grotesk**
- Type: Headings: Bricolage Grotesque 800 (opsz 96) at 88px, tracking 0; body: Bricolage Grotesque 400 (opsz 14) 18px/1.6; captions and prices: Sometype Mono 400 15px
- Palette: bg #FFFFFF (paper), text #151515 (ballpen), accent #E0422F (correction-red, prices and links), surface #F3F1EC (sketchbook), line #CFCBC2
- Layout: 12-col grid; portfolio as a ragged-row grid of mixed aspect ratios with a caption under each; shop as a plain 4-column product list, 0 radius; top nav "Work, Shop, Art for sale, Commissions, About"; home leads with one large recent commission at 8/12 plus a short list of what is for sale at 4/12
- Imagery: flat scans on white, no mockups or frames; process shots of pencil roughs next to finals
- Motion: none on scroll; image opens in the core lightbox; link underline thickens on hover (inside prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference)
- Why it fits now: illustrators are dropping Behance-style grids for plain, fast sites where the drawings do the talking and buying is one email away ([Tom Gauld: Portfolio](https://www.tomgauld.com/portfolio))
**Style variations:** Night desk: #141414 bg, #F2EFE8 text, Blue pencil: #2B4C9B accent on white, Newsprint: #ECE9E1 bg, #111 text
**Refs:**
- [Tom Gauld: Art for Sale](https://www.tomgauld.com/art-for-sale): originals listed with medium, sizes, one price and a RESERVED state; buying instructions in plain words

#### 002 Painter: Studio & Available Works (`oil`)
**For:** painters who sell originals directly and through galleries and need a searchable record of their whole output.
**Templates:** front-page, archive-work (all works, filterable), taxonomy-medium (paintings / drawings / prints / monotypes), single-work, archive-exhibition, single-exhibition, page-about (CV), page-journal, page-contact

**Content model:** `work` (year, medium, dimensions_cm, dimensions_in, series, subject, price, availability available/unavailable/sold, gallery); `exhibition` (dates, venue, type solo/group). Taxonomies `medium`, `series`, `subject`.
**Plugins:** Query Filter (medium / series / subject / availability), Advanced Query Loop (current vs past exhibitions), Jetpack Forms. WooCommerce optional for works sold direct.
**Signature:** a complete works archive filterable by medium, subject, series, availability, price range and year, where every work page shows size in both cm and inches and a plain Available / Unavailable line (seen on: [Tom Hammick: Paintings](https://tomhammick.com/work/paintings)).

- Trust & proof: sold works stay online with a "Sold" label and their last price, so the archive doubles as a price record (seen on: [Tom Climent: Paintings](https://tomcliment.com/product-category/paintings/)).
- Content the owner gets for free: one-click CV PDF generated from the exhibitions and collections data, plus a bibliography page (seen on: [Tom Climent: Bibliography](https://tomcliment.com/bibliography/)).
- Conversion helper: "Enquire about this work" form pre-filled with title, year and size, sent to the studio or the representing gallery.

**Real-world basis:** [Tom Hammick: Paintings](https://tomhammick.com/work/paintings), [Tom Hammick: In Home](https://tomhammick.com/work/in-home), [Tom Climent: Paintings](https://tomcliment.com/product-category/paintings/), [Tom Climent: Kingdom Map](https://tomcliment.com/product/kingdom-map/), [Hughie O'Donoghue: Exhibitions](https://www.hughieodonoghue.com/exhibitions2-1)

- Layout: 12-col grid; works index as a dense grid grouped under large year headings, filters in a left column at 3/12 on desktop and a drawer on mobile; work page puts the painting at 8/12 and the caption block at 4/12, 0 radius; nav "Work, Exhibitions, Journal, About, Contact"

**Style variations:** Salon: #2D2A26 bg, #F2EEE6 text, Linen: #EDE8DC bg, #3D3A33 text, Ultramarine: #1F3A8A accent on white
**Refs:**
- [Tom Hammick: Paintings](https://tomhammick.com/work/paintings): year-grouped works archive with medium, subject, series, availability and price filters

#### 041 Film & Game Composer (`score`)
**For:** media composers working across games, film, TV and concert music.
**Templates:** front-page, page-projects-games, page-projects-film-tv, single-project, page-albums, page-concert-music, page-awards, page-press, page-about, page-contact
**Patterns (~30):** featured project header with "soundtrack available now" links, credits list by year (year, title, platform, studio, publisher, role), cover-art grid of featured projects, albums split by games / film & TV / miscellaneous, works tabs (Solo / Collaboration / Film & art), concert pieces list, awards list, press quotes, Bandcamp and streaming links, store link.
**Content model:** `project` (medium, year, platform, studio, publisher, role, soundtrack links, cue audio). `album` (type, year, label, links).
**Plugins:** none.
**Signature:** a credits list by year in reverse order, each line giving title, platform, studio (linked), publisher and the composer's role (seen on: [Austin Wintory: Videogames](https://www.austinwintory.com/projects-videogames)).
**Nice-to-haves:**
- Albums separated from credits, grouped by games, film & TV and miscellaneous (seen on: [Austin Wintory: Albums, games](https://www.austinwintory.com/albums-games))
- Concert music section for commissions and live shows of game scores (seen on: [Austin Wintory: Concert pieces](https://www.austinwintory.com/concert-pieces))
- Works tabs splitting solo, collaboration and film/art work (seen on: [Ólafur Arnalds: Works](https://olafurarnalds.com/works/))
- Bandcamp vinyl and merch links beside each soundtrack (seen on: [Jessica Curry](https://www.jessicacurry.co.uk/))
- A playlist of short cues built on core Audio, so producers can listen without leaving the page
**Real-world basis:** [Austin Wintory: Videogames](https://www.austinwintory.com/projects-videogames), [Ólafur Arnalds: Works](https://olafurarnalds.com/works/), [Jessica Curry](https://www.jessicacurry.co.uk/), [Anna Meredith](https://www.annameredith.com/)
**Style: Score-page serif (engraved titles, cue-sheet tables)**
- Type: headings Castoro Titling 400 at 84px, body Castoro 400 at 18px/1.6, credits table Source Sans 3 500 at 16px with tabular figures.
- Palette: bg #F6F5F1, text #1B1B1B, accent #1F4E79 (manuscript ink blue, for listen and links), surface #EAE8E1, line #B9B4A8.
- Layout: credits as a real table grouped by year headings; featured work as a 3/9 split with cover art left and notes right; staves are not used as decoration; square corners, 1px rules.
- Imagery: official key art for games and films at native ratio; studio photos of the composer at the desk or with players.
- Motion: none; audio only plays on click and never autoplays.
- Why it fits now: producers and game studios hire from credit lists, and a serious serif with clean tables reads as established without film-trailer clichés (example: [Anna Meredith](https://www.annameredith.com/)).
**Style variations:** Synth: #101418 bg, #7FD1C7 accent, Orchestral: #F3EFE6 bg, #6B1F1F accent, Pencil: greyscale only
**Refs:**
- [Austin Wintory: Videogames](https://www.austinwintory.com/projects-videogames): cover grid of featured games above a complete year-by-year credits list with studio and role.
- [Ólafur Arnalds: Works](https://olafurarnalds.com/works/): works split into solo, collaboration and film/art tabs with year and listen links.
- [Jessica Curry](https://www.jessicacurry.co.uk/): composer site that places games, projects, presenting and a store side by side.

#### 042 Classical Ensemble / Chamber Group (`quartet`)
**For:** chamber ensembles, early-music groups and choirs that run a season, tour, record and do learning work.
**Templates:** front-page, archive-event (diary), single-event, taxonomy-city, archive-recording (discography), page-editions, page-musicians, page-learning, archive-learning-project, page-support, page-about, page-contact
**Patterns (~40):** diary grouped by month (date, time, venue with address, city, programme), city tags (Edinburgh / Glasgow / Perth / London), concert page with programme set as composer in bold then work title, booking link to the venue, "Add to calendar" (Google, iCal, Outlook), relaxed performance and open rehearsal tags, musicians list, discography with label and catalogue number, performing editions page, learning projects (e.g. a children's Messiah), support/friends scheme, news.
**Content model:** `concert` (datetime, venue, city taxonomy, programme rows: composer + work + optional year, performers, ticket_url, tags). `recording` (label, date, cat_no, links). `musician` (instrument).
**Plugins:** The Events Calendar (iCal export and month grouping), GiveWP (friends scheme).
**Signature:** a programme block that sets each work as composer in bold followed by the work title, used on every concert page (seen on: [Hebrides Ensemble: Scotland's composers 1](https://www.hebridesensemble.com/events/event/wigmore-hall-scotlands-composers-1/)).
**Nice-to-haves:**
- Calendar export (Google, iCal, Outlook) on the diary (seen on: [Dunedin Consort: Diary](https://www.dunedin-consort.org.uk/diary/))
- Relaxed performances and open rehearsals tagged in the diary (seen on: [Dunedin Consort: Diary](https://www.dunedin-consort.org.uk/diary/))
- City tags so audiences in each town can see only their concerts (seen on: [Dunedin Consort: Edinburgh](https://www.dunedin-consort.org.uk/diary/tag/edinburgh/))
- Learning project page with dates for schools and families (seen on: [Dunedin Consort: Children's Messiah](https://www.dunedin-consort.org.uk/learning-and-participation/childrens-messiah/))
- Discography with label and catalogue number for each album, e.g. Signum SIGCD938 (seen on: [Fretwork: Discography](https://www.fretwork.co.uk/discography))
**Real-world basis:** [Hebrides Ensemble: Events](https://www.hebridesensemble.com/events/), [Hebrides Ensemble: Scotland's composers 1](https://www.hebridesensemble.com/events/event/wigmore-hall-scotlands-composers-1/), [Dunedin Consort: Diary](https://www.dunedin-consort.org.uk/diary/), [Dunedin Consort: Editions](https://www.dunedin-consort.org.uk/editions/), [Fretwork](https://www.fretwork.co.uk/)
**Style: Concert-hall bill (display serif, strict programme columns)**
- Type: headings Libre Caslon Display 400 at 88px, body Karla 400 at 18px/1.6, composer names Karla 700 at 18px, dates Karla 500 tabular.
- Palette: bg #FFFFFF, text #1A1A1A, accent #7A1F2B (hall claret, for booking links), surface #F3F1EC, line #1A1A1A.
- Layout: diary as month headings with rows (date / venue and city / programme / book); concert page as a 5/7 split with facts left and programme right; programme rows aligned so composer names form a column; square corners, 1px rules.
- Imagery: photos of the players in rehearsal and in the venue, landscape 3:2; no instrument clip-art.
- Motion: none; month headings stay sticky while scrolling the diary, inside prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference.
- Why it fits now: ensemble audiences scan by date, town and programme, and a printed-bill layout with a strong serif carries the tradition without looking dated (example: [Dunedin Consort: Diary](https://www.dunedin-consort.org.uk/diary/)).
**Style variations:** Baroque: #F2EEE4 bg, #5A3E1B accent, Modern: #FFFFFF bg, #0F4C81 accent, Night concert: #16161A bg, #F3F1EC type
**Refs:**
- [Dunedin Consort: Diary](https://www.dunedin-consort.org.uk/diary/): WordPress diary grouped by month with city tags, calendar exports, relaxed performances and open rehearsals.
- [Hebrides Ensemble: Scotland's composers 1](https://www.hebridesensemble.com/events/event/wigmore-hall-scotlands-composers-1/): concert page with the programme set as composer then work, venue address and booking link.
- [Fretwork: Discography](https://www.fretwork.co.uk/discography): album covers with label catalogue numbers and buy/stream links.
