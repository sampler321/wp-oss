**Templates:** front-page, page-download, page-waitlist, page-early-access, page-transparency, page-students, page-nonprofits, archive-release, page-faq, page-privacy
**Patterns (~30):** per-platform availability (download for Mac / join the Windows waitlist), waitlist form with what happens next, early access programme page, release notes, data and model transparency page (what is stored, for how long, whether it trains models), student and nonprofit offers, status link, FAQ, privacy policy, founders note
**Content model:** `platform` (name, status: available / waitlist / planned, download URL, waitlist form); `release`.
**Plugins:** Jetpack Forms (waitlist), Jetpack Newsletter (launch emails).
**Signature:** per-platform download buttons where unreleased platforms turn into "join the waitlist" with their own form (seen on: [Dia](https://www.diabrowser.com/windows)).
**Nice-to-haves:**
- Transparency page on data retention and model training (seen on: [Granola](https://www.granola.ai/transparency))
- Student offer page (seen on: [Granola](https://www.granola.ai/students))
- Early access programme page (seen on: [Dia](https://www.diabrowser.com/earlybirds))
- "Latest release notes" permalink (seen on: [Dia](https://www.diabrowser.com/release-notes/latest))
- Confirmation page that tells people what happens after they join the waitlist and roughly when, instead of a bare "thanks"
**Style: Quiet e-paper minimal**
- Type: headings Rowan (Fontshare) 500 at large sizes; body General Sans (Fontshare) 400/18px; version and dates Fragment Mono 400
- Palette: bg #EFEDE8 (e-paper grey), text #1D1B18, accent #1D1B18 (ink only; the product screenshot carries the colour), surface #FFFFFF, line #CFCBC2
- Layout: short front page: one sentence on what the tool does, a real screenshot or 20-second screen recording, then the platform list; long text lives on the transparency and FAQ pages
- Imagery: real product captures only; no sparkles, orbs or robot art
- Motion: the screen recording plays muted with controls, paused under reduced motion; nothing else animates
- Why it fits now: calmer, paper-like sites have become the counter-signal to loud AI launches (example: [Daylight Computer](https://daylightcomputer.com/))
**Style variations:** Ink: #1D1B18 bg, paper text, Graphite: #D9D7D2 ground, Signal: one brand colour for the download button only
**Real-world basis:** [Dia: Windows](https://www.diabrowser.com/windows), [Dia: early birds](https://www.diabrowser.com/earlybirds), [Granola: transparency](https://www.granola.ai/transparency), [Granola: nonprofits](https://www.granola.ai/nonprofits), [Daylight Computer: FAQ](https://daylightcomputer.com/faq)
**Refs:**
- [Dia: Windows](https://www.diabrowser.com/windows): one platform live, one waitlisted, both clear
- [Granola: transparency](https://www.granola.ai/transparency): the data questions answered before sign-up
- [Zed: compare](https://zed.dev/compare): comparisons written for a technical audience

#### 159 Freelance Developer / Consultant (`consult`)
**For:** independent developers, accessibility consultants and technical writers who sell their time, talks and workshops.
**Templates:** front-page, page-hire, page-projects, page-speaking, page-press-kit, page-endorsements, archive (posts), page-uses, page-colophon, page-newsletter, page-contact
**Patterns (~35):** hire page (services, who they won't work with, contact), projects list, speaking list (events, dates, slides, video), press kit (short and long bio, pronunciation, headshots to download, handles), endorsements split into client stories and workshop quotes, uses page, colophon, newsletter, writing archive, /now or availability line
**Content model:** `project` (client, year, role, link); `talk` (event, date, city, slides URL, video URL); `endorsement` (name, role, company, type: client / workshop).
**Plugins:** Jetpack Newsletter.
**Signature:** a press kit page with short and long bios, a pronunciation guide, social handles and downloadable headshots (seen on: [Sara Soueidan](https://www.sarasoueidan.com/press-kit/)).
**Nice-to-haves:**
- Hire page that states who the consultant does not work with (seen on: [HeydonWorks](https://heydonworks.com/hire))
- Endorsements separated into long client stories and short workshop quotes (seen on: [Sara Soueidan](https://www.sarasoueidan.com/endorsements/))
- Speaking page with past events and slides (seen on: [hidde.blog](https://hidde.blog/speaking/))
- Uses page listing tools (seen on: [Zach Leatherman](https://www.zachleat.com/uses/))
- "Booking work from February" availability line in the header, set in one place
**Style: Kinetic variable-type homepage**
- Type: Sono variable for everything, 800 for headings, proportional (MONO 0) and 400 for body at 18px, MONO 1 for code and dates
- Palette: bg #FFFFFF, text #111111, accent #FFE14D (highlighter), surface #F4F4F4, line #0B57D0 (link blue)
- Layout: personal, asymmetric page with a large name set in the casual cut and a plain list of what they do; projects and talks as dated lists; press kit as a download grid with file sizes
- Imagery: honest portraits, conference photos, and screenshots of their own work; no stock desks
- Motion: the casual axis eases between 0 and 1 on hover over the name only; off under reduced motion
- Why it fits now: independent web people have kept personal, hand-built homepages as their main calling card (example: [Miriam Eric Suzanne](https://www.miriamsuzanne.com/))
**Style variations:** Midnight: #111 bg, yellow links, Marker: #FF6FAE highlighter, Plain: black on white, blue links only
**Real-world basis:** [HeydonWorks: hire](https://heydonworks.com/hire), [Sara Soueidan: press kit](https://www.sarasoueidan.com/press-kit/), [Sara Soueidan: endorsements](https://www.sarasoueidan.com/endorsements/), [Zach Leatherman: projects](https://www.zachleat.com/projects/), [hidde.blog: speaking](https://hidde.blog/speaking/)
**Refs:**
- [Sara Soueidan: press kit](https://www.sarasoueidan.com/press-kit/): everything an event organiser needs on one page
- [HeydonWorks: hire](https://heydonworks.com/hire): services and ethics stated plainly
- [Miriam Eric Suzanne: colophon](https://www.miriamsuzanne.com/colophon/): a personal site that explains how it is made

#### 160 Product Changelog & Public Roadmap Hub (`roadmap`)
**For:** small software teams that want a changelog, public roadmap and feature request list on their own domain instead of a hosted board.
**Templates:** front-page, archive-release, single-release, page-roadmap, archive-request, single-request, page-submit-request, page-subscribe, page-changelog-rss
**Patterns (~35):** changelog entry (date, title, image or video, fixes list, improvements list), change types (added / changed / deprecated / removed / fixed / security), roadmap in three sections (now / next / community requests), request list by status (pending / added / fixed / not possible / not planning) with vote counts and dates, reasons shown for declined requests, subscribe by email and RSS, version anchors, link to docs from each entry
**Content model:** `release` (version, date, type, change groups); `request` (status, votes, submitted date, decision reason, linked release).
**Plugins:** Jetpack Newsletter (email on new release), Jetpack Forms (request submission).
**Signature:** a feature request list grouped by status that keeps "not planning to implement" items visible with the reason, plus vote counts and dates (seen on: [Perfmatters](https://perfmatters.io/docs/feature-requests/)).
**Nice-to-haves:**
- Roadmap split into "currently working on", "coming up next" and community items with votes (seen on: [Zed](https://zed.dev/roadmap))
- Change groups that follow Keep a Changelog, with linkable versions and dates (seen on: [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/))
- Fixes and improvements as lists under each feature announcement (seen on: [Linear](https://linear.app/changelog))
- Request marked "shipped" links to the release that shipped it, via the linked release meta
- RSS feed per product area for customers who only care about one part
**Style: Departure board status list**
- Type: headings Jersey 10 400; body Onest 400/18px; versions, dates and vote counts JetBrains Mono 500
- Palette: bg #FFFFFF, text #111111, accent #F2A900 (in progress), surface #F3F3F3, line #0A7F3F (shipped); #9B1C1C used only for "not planning"
- Layout: roadmap as a departures-style table (item, status, updated), statuses as text with a coloured square, never a pulsing dot; changelog as a dated column with version anchors in the margin
- Imagery: product screenshots in changelog entries only, at native size
- Motion: none; status changes are instant
- Why it fits now: teams are moving roadmaps back onto their own sites as plain, linkable lists (example: [Zed roadmap](https://zed.dev/roadmap))
**Style variations:** Night board: #111 bg, amber text, Paper log: #FBFAF6 ground, Mono: black and white, status as words only
**Real-world basis:** [Perfmatters: feature requests](https://perfmatters.io/docs/feature-requests/), [Zed: roadmap](https://zed.dev/roadmap), [Linear: changelog](https://linear.app/changelog), [Plausible: changelog](https://plausible.io/changelog), [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/)
**Refs:**
- [Perfmatters: feature requests](https://perfmatters.io/docs/feature-requests/): statuses, votes and reasons for declining
- [Zed: roadmap](https://zed.dev/roadmap): now, next and community-voted items
- [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/): the change types this theme's release blocks should follow
