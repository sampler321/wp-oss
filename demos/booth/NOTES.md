# Booth (Dźwig)

- Direction: "actually modern", after studionagrywarka.pl. Pale concrete grey page, one very large black headline sitting on top of a loose collage of room photos, then a full-bleed record-light orange block for the rooms, rounded photos (18px) and 8px buttons. The research's industrial gear list stays as the working part of the site, restyled as a clean inventory.
- Fonts: Figtree only (claimed in demos/booth/fonts-claim.txt; the brief moves away from the registry's Tektur): 900 for headlines at up to 10rem, 400/600/700 for text. No mono.
- Palette: #F3F3F1 concrete, #0A0A0A black, #FF5B24 record light (full-bleed blocks with black text on them, "Shared" gear badges), #D63F0B for small accents on grey. Nagrywarka's lilac was not copied because it sits in the banned violet band.
- Signature: gear inventory by category (consoles, microphones, recorders, outboard, instruments) with a sticky index, a room badge per item (A, B or shared), a "we can hire what is missing" note and a text-only gear list shipped as assets/gear-list.txt.
- Nice-to-haves as patterns: weekly "next free dates" bar (template part), deposit and cancellation terms beside the rates, load-in, parking and nearest hardware shop, rehearsal room, engineers with credits.
- Core-block limits: the headline-over-collage layout uses a CSS grid in theme.json styles.css (grid areas and top offsets); on phones it becomes headline then two photos.
- Photos are CC0 stand-ins from other studios; swap in your own rooms.
