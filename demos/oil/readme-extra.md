Oil is for painters who want their whole output online as a catalogue: every painting, drawing and monotype, sold ones included, with medium, year, size in cm and inches, and a plain availability line.

To add a work, write a post, set its featured image to the painting, put it in a medium category (Paintings, Drawings, Monotypes) and tag it with its subject and its availability (Available, Sold or On loan). Insert the "Work caption" pattern and fill in the medium, size and price. Choose the "Work (painting and caption)" template. Works tagged Sold get a red dot in the index.

The front page and the Work page list everything newest first, beside an index of mediums, years and tags. Exhibitions, Journal, About (with CV blocks) and Contact are built from page patterns.
