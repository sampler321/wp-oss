# Drop: notes

- Direction: a 90s photocopied zine taped to a wall, for a band (demo: Wet Static, Leeds) selling its own merch. The owner asked for Nirvana-style grunge with the band Julie as reference, so the research's streetwear mono look was dropped. Julie's Bandcamp (sage grey wall, scratchy ballpoint drawings, collaged scans) set the palette and the handwriting. No real band artwork or logo is used.
- Fonts: Reenie Beanie (display, ballpoint scrawl, claimed in fonts-claim.txt) and Cutive (body, a proportional typewriter-style serif). Two families, no monospace.
- Palette: photocopy grey #C5CBBE, toner black, paper scrap off-white, ballpoint blue for handwritten notes and counts, stamp red only for sold out.
- Signature: the typed stock sheet with real counts written in ballpoint ("6 left", "ships 24 to 29 Nov"), sold-out items struck through and kept on the sheet. Pre-orders are labelled in production with a batch window; "missed out" and batch-two notes; sold-out record of past drops as posts.
- Distinctive bits: paper scraps with masking tape and a slight tilt (section styles), every photo forced to high-contrast greyscale and multiplied onto the paper with a CSS filter, headings set lowercase.
- Nice-to-haves as patterns: second batch note, sold-out record, region-only edition, size guide with the tee-in-your-drawer trick, pre-orders ship separately, last posting dates notice.
- Core-block limits: the stock sheet is hand-maintained text; live counts show in the WooCommerce shop and product pages.
