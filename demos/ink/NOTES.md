# ink: notes

Direction: a plain, fast illustrator's site where the drawings do the talking. Bricolage Grotesque only (800 for headings, 400 for text), paper white, ballpen black, correction red for links and prices, a sketchbook beige surface and pencil-line rules. Joon Park, gouache and pencil, Jamaica Street, Bristol.

Signature: the Art for sale page. Every original has medium, drawing and sheet size, one flat price or a Reserved/Sold label, then three lines on how to buy by email and a postage table.

Core-block limits: the lightbox can't be put on a Query Loop featured image, so archive grids link to the project page, and the lightbox lives on the hand-placed grids, galleries and post images. The masonry wall is a CSS `columns` section style (`is-style-masonry`) on a group, gallery or post template.

## Round 2

Owner feedback: "why does it have such a giant image on page 1 and start with a pricing table", "way too many tables", "I don't see any lightboxes. See zosiapaszkiet.com", plus the general brief. Studied zosiapaszkiet.com (home, works, about), tomgauld.com (news, art for sale), jeanjullien.com (works) and ellensurrey.com (portfolio, process, commissions).

- **Home page rebuilt around the work.** A short intro (h1 "Illustration by Joon Park", one line about the work, three links: prints and books, originals, commissions) and then straight into a 12-piece masonry wall like Ellen Surrey's. Every image opens in the lightbox, and the caption under it links to the project. After that: recent pieces (the latest six posts), three ways in (shop, originals, commissions), a sketchbook strip, a client testimonial, the commissions-open box and a newsletter footer. The home page has no prices and no table.
- **Lightbox on everywhere.** `settings.blocks["core/image"].lightbox` is on in theme.json. All portfolio images, galleries, process strips, series plates and post images use it. Gallery captions now sit under the image instead of over it.
- **Tables: 2 of 70 patterns** (postage by region, and the commission price list, both really tabular). The originals list, work details, exhibitions, press, events and print specs are now hairline "spec rows" made from groups. Commission options are priced cards. Books are cover cards.
- **Kit grew from 33 to 70 patterns**, in registered categories (portfolio, shop, commissions, events, about, contact, testimonials, call-to-action, posts, footer, pages): masonry, even grid, one large column, two-up, featured piece, series plates, project brief, project result, process strip, sketchbook strip, detail crops, category links, shop highlights, print detail, print scale, originals grid and list, books, commission options/steps/price list/FAQ/terms/past work/open/closed, testimonial, press list and quotes, clients, events list, workshops, open studio, four about and four contact variants, two newsletters, three footers, a 404 and nine page layouts.
- **Templates and parts are generated from build/ink.py.** New `category.html`, a reworked `single-work` (breadcrumb, large picture, details, previous/next, recent pieces), `single` for studio diary posts, a proper 404 with a search, and three footer parts (studio, newsletter band, one line). The header keeps the nav and the WooCommerce icons on the right.
- **Demo:** 18 works as posts with featured images, across Editorial, Books, Posters and murals, Packaging and pattern, and Maps. Four are full project stories (brief, process strip, result with client quote), and Hummingbirds is a six-plate series page. There are also three studio diary posts, and new pages for Selected work, Sketchbook, Prints, Events and workshops, and Press. Every template is reachable, and the Pattern library is linked in the menu.
- **Scale:** display and heading sizes came down (h1 now 2.1 to 3rem, h2 1.5 to 2rem). Body stays at 18px. Images: 14 more public-domain plates (Audubon, Haeckel, Hokusai, Hiroshige, Ohara Koson, Redoute), all credited in readme.txt.
