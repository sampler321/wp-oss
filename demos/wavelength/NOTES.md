# Wavelength (Calder Valley Radio 104.6 FM)

- Why this station: freq is already a music-led internet station with a Swiss index look, and bpm is one DJ's night archive, so the second radio theme is a licensed local community FM station: talk, hourly news, a noticeboard read out on air, Town Hall Talk, hear-again programmes and 70 volunteers. That audience is mixed and often older, which sets the direction: civic, warm and plain-language, like a good council leaflet rather than a music brand.
- Look: warm paper #FFFBF0, moor green #1F3A2E, brick red #B83A1A for links and "on now", sun yellow #F5C542 as a noticeboard background only. Rounded buttons, 12px photos, 2px underlined links, a bus-timetable schedule and a yellow noticeboard with pinned notice cards.
- Fonts: Parkinsans for headings (claimed in demos/wavelength/fonts-claim.txt; drawn for a health charity, friendly and very legible) and Atkinson Hyperlegible Next for text at 19px. No mono.
- Signature: an FM tuning dial (88 to 108) across the top of every page with a yellow needle on 104.6, then a front page that answers "what is on now" and "how do I listen" before anything else.
- Variations: Evening (dark green), Mill town (slate and blue), Large print (black on white with every font size stepped up), because some listeners will want it.
- Nice-to-haves as patterns: transmitter work notice with times, listening help as questions, volunteer roles with time needed, open evenings, how the station is paid for, complaints and corrections, a call for local bands.
- Core-block limits: the dial is a group with CSS tick marks (repeating gradients) and the needle is a pseudo-element, so moving it to another frequency means editing one percentage in the section style. Hear-again audio URLs are placeholders.
