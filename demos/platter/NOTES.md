# Platter: notes

- Direction: the kitchen pass. Loud slab type, tomato, mustard and pickle green, close hot food photos. The owner asked for tastier, less elegant and more fun, so the research's austere white look was dropped. Demo: Second Helpings, an event caterer in St Werburghs, Bristol.
- Fonts: Bevan (display, heavy slab with a real italic, claimed in fonts-claim.txt) and Karla (body, tabular figures for prices and covers). No monospace.
- Palette: white, espresso brown ink, tomato red, mustard, pickle green, custard for the printed menu card.
- Signature: the ticket rail. Case studies are posts shown as white kitchen tickets with a torn zigzag bottom on a tomato band, each with venue, guest count, format and what went out; category archives (weddings, crew, offices, dinners at home) act as the filter.
- Other distinctive bits: round tomato price stickers, a gingham tablecloth band behind the enquiry brief, a centred canapé card with a double rule.
- Nice-to-haves as patterns: seasonal menu with its date window, brochure request, named suppliers with distances, office lunch cut-off note, allergen matrix.
- Core-block limits: there is no core form block, so the event brief is a numbered list and a prefilled email button.
