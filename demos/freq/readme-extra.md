Freq is for community, internet and freeform radio stations run by volunteers. A black live player bar is fixed to the bottom of every page: put your stream URL in its Audio block and update the now and next lines. The week is a seven-column schedule grid with the station timezone printed on it; the show on air is marked with a red square.

Shows are post categories (the category description becomes the show's intro) and episodes are posts, each with an Audio or Embed block and a tracklist. Assign the "Episode" template to episode posts. Patterns cover on air now, a text index of recent episodes, black and white episode tiles, a residents grid, a shows table, a support block that says what donations pay for, membership levels, submit a show, a guest mix series, ways to listen, an off-air notice with an end time, and finding the studio.
