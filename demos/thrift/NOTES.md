# thrift: notes

- Direction: a 1950s workwear mail-order catalogue for a one-off vintage shop. Canvas-grey pages, black condensed caps, double catalogue rules, ruled measurement tables, and prices on red swing tags with a punched hole drawn in CSS.
- Signature: the decade index (40s, 50s, 60s, 70s, 80s, 90s, Y2K) set as huge Barlow Condensed numerals across the full width as the home hero, each linking to that decade's product category, plus a size guide that explains label size against flat measurements.
- Every piece has four flat measurements (pit to pit, shoulder, nape to hem, shoulder to cuff) in cm and inches, a condition line and a flaw line. Sold pieces stay listed with a black SOLD tag. New-in tiles swap to the back view of the garment on hover or focus (a second photo, no zoom).
- Fonts: Barlow Condensed 500/700/800 (display, from the registry) and Barlow 400/500/600 (body). No mono; tabular lining figures in tables and tags.
- Palette: canvas #EDEDE8, ink #1D1F1C, swing-tag red #B5471B, white card #FFFFFF, khaki #C9BB93. Variations: Y2K (silver, pink tags), 70s (mustard and brown), Military (olive).
- Demo images: US Army garments photographed flat by a French military museum (CC0), a MET silk blouse, a Rijksmuseum smock dress, and CC0 Unsplash shop photos.
- Core-block limits: product attributes (decade, measurements) live in product categories and the description table because the demo builder makes simple products only. The back-view hover works in patterns; the WooCommerce product grid shows one image.
