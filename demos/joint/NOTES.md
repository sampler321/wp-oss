# joint: notes

- Direction: "workshop drawing sheet". Pages read like sheets from the makers' plan chest: measured drawings as product images inside white plates with a black keyline, thin rules, and a boxed title block (timber, W / D / H in mm and inches, lead time) wherever a real drawing would have one.
- Fonts: Familjen Grotesk (registry face) for headings, labels and navigation; Newsreader for text. Numbers use tabular figures.
- Palette: planed ash #F5F1EA, pencil #231F1A, oiled green #3E5641 for links and actions, shaving #E8E1D5, rule #BFB3A0. Square corners throughout.
- Signature: `joint/source-book`, a swatch library of eight British timbers (square samples cropped from one CC0 sample board), each with its sawmill and one line on how it behaves; the Materials page adds finishes, complementary materials and a "come and see the wood" visit block.
- Also: product sheet (7/5 split, price per timber table), lead-time lines (in stock 1 to 5 days, made to order 6 to 12 weeks), made-to-size note, care and ten-year guarantee, refinishing service, commission process, trade enquiries, stool-making course (sold as a Woo product), collaborations list.
- WooCommerce: eight simple products. Variable products (timber by finish) are not supported by the demo builder, so timber prices are shown in a table on the product sheet pattern.
- Core-block limits: the timber-switch crossfade from the research needs JS and was left out. Product-to-material links are plain links to the Materials page.
- Images are CC0 Index of American Design drawings (NGA) and Wikimedia photos standing in for the makers' own drawings.

## Round 2
- Object pages are now image first: the drawing at full width, then name and price, three detail images (lightbox), the story, the piece in use at full bleed, sizes as big dimension callouts (mm with inches), timbers as rows with round wood samples and prices, how it is made, care, and other pieces. No tables on object pages.
- Four object pages under /pieces/ (Calder chair, Aire desk, Heptonstall side table, Crag bench), built from the object blocks; the first uses the patterns directly.
- Home opens with the newest piece (drawing, name, sizes) instead of a title block table, then the pieces index, a room with a piece in it, a four-step making strip with pictures, the timber library and projects.
- Projects are told as drawing plus story plus facts in a boxed title block made of rows. Finishes, collaborations and course details are rows, not tables. Three tables remain: a spec sheet, the in-stock list and delivery rates.
- 51 patterns in nine registered categories. Lightbox on. Added public-domain engravings (dovetail, holdfast, planes) and a plane-and-shavings photo for the making sections.
