Spine is an author website built on the horizontal three-band paperback grid: a colour band at the top, a pale panel in the middle and a colour band at the foot. The colour follows the genre: orange for fiction, green for crime, dark blue for memoir and cerise for travel. Each book is a post in its genre category; assign it the matching "Book" template (Book: crime, Book: memoir and so on) and the header, footer and cover band switch to that colour. Category archives for crime, memoir and travel have their own coloured templates.

Covers on the home page and books page are drawn with blocks (band, panel, band), so a new book needs no cover scan. The book page uses the later photo-cover layout: a genre band with the title above a duotone photograph set as the featured image.

Patterns include a new-book hero with one buy button, a shelf of covers, a buy block that lists the local independent shop and Bookshop.org before the big retailers, signed copies from one named shop, international editions and translations, books in reading order, praise quotes, a first-page excerpt, a book-group guide, events, a newsletter sign-up with past issues, and contacts split between agent, publicist and film rights.

The typefaces are Cabin, a humanist sans in the Johnston and Gill tradition, and Literata. The theme has no publisher logo or other trade dress; only the grid and the colour code.
