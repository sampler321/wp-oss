# Spine

- Direction (owner's brief: Penguin aesthetics): the whole site is a paperback. Header band, pale panel, footer band, in the genre colour: orange fiction, green crime, dark blue memoir, cerise travel. No logo, no bird, no cartouche; only the horizontal grid and the colour code.
- Fonts: Cabin (the 051 registry face, a humanist sans in the Johnston and Gill line) for titles, nav and labels; Literata for reading. Two families, no mono.
- Signature: flat covers drawn with blocks (band, panel with title and rule, band) on a strict shelf; the new-book hero with exactly one "Buy the book" button; book pages in the later photo-cover grid (genre band with the title over a duotone photograph in the genre colour); the ruled buy list with the local indie shop and Bookshop.org before the big retailers.
- How the colour switches: each book post gets a "Book: crime / fiction / memoir / travel" template, which uses matching header and footer template parts; crime, memoir and travel category archives have their own templates. No PHP, no custom fields.
- Pages: books in order, buy (all editions, signed copies from one named shop, international editions and translations), events, book groups (questions and guide), newsletter with an archive of past letters, about, contact split by agent, publicist and film rights.
- Variations: Hardback (cloth grey, gold-brown), Pulp (yellow, red), Crime at night (dark).
- No author portrait: Commons had no suitable CC0 portrait, so the about page uses a desk photo with an honest caption. Praise quotes are attributed to invented publications and people.
