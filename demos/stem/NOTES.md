# stem: notes

- Direction: the owner asked for more style and colour, so this florist is colour-blocked. Every bouquet sits on its own saturated field (marigold, cobalt, mint, leaf green, poppy) like a studio still life on seasonal paper, on a petal-pink page, with a fat flared serif set big and tight like a flower-market poster.
- Signature: the same-day cut-off is the home headline ("Order by 2pm, on the table by 6.") on a poppy field, repeated under every bouquet and on every product, with delivery zones and charges, a "missed the cut-off? call us" line, the substitution sentence and a gift message note.
- Also built: this week's flowers as a list with names in colour, sizes explained, subscriptions (weekly or monthly, prepaid or rolling, saving stated), real weddings as posts with flowers used and photographer credits, a workshops calendar with places left, a printable care card, and an occasion cut-off bar for Mother's Day and Valentine's.
- Fonts: Chonburi (display, claimed; Alice was too gentle for "style and colour heavy") and Rethink Sans 400 to 800 (body). Two families, no mono.
- Palette: petal pink #FFE3EA, aubergine #2B0B1E, poppy #C4221B, marigold #FFC72C, cobalt #1D4ED8, mint #BFEBD6, leaf #1F6B3A, cream #FFF6E9. The WooCommerce grid cycles the same five fields behind product photos. Variations: Dried (sand and rust), Studio (white, one colour), Night (near-black, ivory).
- Core-block limit: WordPress applies one block style variation per block, so the per-bouquet colour fields use the Group's background and text colour presets, and the section style only handles the still-life layout.
- The delivery-date picker and subscriptions plugins from the research are not part of the theme; the copy and patterns cover the rules in plain words.
