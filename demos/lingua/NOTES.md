# Lingua: notes

- Direction: a parallel text. Important statements are set twice, English left and Portuguese right in italic, split by a hairline, so the translator's own writing is the sample. Proofreader's red is the only colour.
- Persona: Joanna Pryce, Portuguese and Spanish into English, in Porto since 2011. Literary, academic, museum and wine texts.
- Fonts: Noto Serif Display (display, the registry face) and Noto Sans (body). Two families, no monospace.
- Palette: paper white, ink #111111, proof red #B5121B, galley grey surface, pencil line, a highlighter yellow for the "booked until" status line.
- Signature: the opening statement in both languages, a proof sheet where a machine translation is struck through and corrected in red beside the source, and a bibliography list of published translations (posts with a small cover image).
- Bilingual site: without Polylang the theme gives a full page in Portuguese ("Em português", linked from the EN / PT switch in the header) plus parallel columns everywhere. A real mirror of every page needs a multilingual plugin. The accented nav label came out garbled from the demo builder, so the PT link sits in the header and footer instead of the menu.
- The "Parallel text" Columns style works anywhere; responsive rules for it live in theme.json styles.css because section-style CSS flattens @media.
