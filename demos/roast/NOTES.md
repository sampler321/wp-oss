# Roast (Lintel Coffee)

- Direction: as researched, "kraft-label utility". A Liverpool roaster whose site reads like the label on its bags: coffee data (producer, region, variety, process, elevation, harvest, price paid) in hard black ruled boxes, tasting notes set big, kraft-paper panels and a stamp-green accent.
- Fonts: Krona One (display, the registry face for 059) and Karla (body, tabular figures for doses, times and prices). No monospace: the brief's Azeret Mono data face was dropped under the new rule, and the timeline times use Krona One instead.
- Palette: white, bean black #1E1A16, stamp green #1F6F4A, roast brown #8A3B12, kraft #D8C3A0, pale kraft #F1E7D6. Variations: Lab, Espresso bar (dark), Seasonal.
- Signature: brew guides as posts (category Brew guides, custom template "Brew guide") with a facts table (dose, water, temperature, grind, time) beside a pour schedule table styled as a timeline (is-style-timeline), plus "if it tastes wrong" fixes.
- Also: coffee label pattern, roast-days bar (template part), Christmas last-roast notice, letterbox note, bag sizes with a sold-out state, prices-we-pay table, producer story, process steps, subscription builder (coffee by frequency), pause/skip/one-off explainer, wholesale price list with PDF link, cafés and hours, gift card.
- Core-block limits: no live brew timer (the research suggested an Interactivity API timer; that would need a custom block). Coffee attributes are written into the product short description; Woo attributes would need to be added per product by the shop.
