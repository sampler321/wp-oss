Kiln is for potters who sell in scheduled shop updates and at kiln-opening sales. Between updates the site says plainly when the shop opens next, keeps every sold pot listed as sold out, and points people to the newsletter.

It needs WooCommerce for the shop. Sold-out products stay visible with a dimmed photo. To change the next update date, edit the "Shop closed: next update" pattern on the front page and the "Shop status bar" in the Header template part. On the day of an update, swap in the "Shop open now" pattern.

Firing notes are posts in the Firings category and appear in the Kiln log. Kiln opening, Process, Stockists and Newsletter pages are built from page patterns.
