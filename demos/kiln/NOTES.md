# kiln: notes

- Direction: glaze-test catalogue as a noticeboard for the next shop update. Colour slabs instead of cards: a full-bleed celadon slab with the next update date set huge, a biscuit panel for the kiln opening timetable, a tenmoku footer.
- Fonts: Zodiak 700 (display, registry) and General Sans (body). Both Fontshare, self-hosted. No third family.
- Palette: porcelain #F4F3EF, clay black #23211E, celadon #2F5E6E, tenmoku #3A2418, plus glaze tokens (shino, ash, cobalt, pale celadon). Variations: Tenmoku (dark), Ash, Cobalt.
- Signature: the shop sits in a closed state (status bar in the header part on every inner page, big slab on the front page), sold pots stay in the shop with a 60% photo and ", sold out" after the price (CSS on Woo's outofstock class), and the newsletter promises the list two days early.
- Glaze key: five square test tiles in palette colours, a pattern that doubles as a legend for the shop.
- Could not do with core blocks: a real open/closed switch or countdown (deliberately none), hover swap to a hand-held photo, "notify me when back" form (mailto instead), per-product firing number field (written into the description).
- Woo templates are left to WooCommerce; they inherit the header with the status bar and the theme tokens.
