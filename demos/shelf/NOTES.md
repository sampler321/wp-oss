# Shelf (De Inktvis, Rotterdam)

- Direction: the owner asked for "penguin!!! or something more illustration-ish, style heavy", with Plato Rotterdam's branding. Penguin is spine's direction, so this leans on Plato: a loud black-and-white shop with one signal red, a round tilted sticker logo (Plato's round avatar), and old public-domain engravings printed in a red duotone as the shop's house illustrations. plato.nl turned out to be an HR consultancy and Instagram is behind a login, so the reference came from the Instagram avatar and platomania.nl (Plato's web shop).
- Fonts: Luckiest Guy (display, hand-cut poster capitals, claimed in demos/shelf/fonts-claim.txt instead of the researched Almendra) and DM Sans (body). No monospace.
- Palette: white, ink #111111, signal red #D7261E, sticker yellow #FFD23F, newsprint #F3EEE4. Duotone presets Red print and Ink print; featured images get Red print by default via theme.json. Hard 6px black offset shadow as the only shadow preset. Variations: Stacks, Basement (dark green), Children's corner.
- Signature: staff picks as yellow shelf-talker cards (posts in the Staff picks category), tilted a degree either way with a hard shadow, plus the book-a-month subscription explainer (questionnaire, pick, post) in a full-width red band with big numbered steps.
- Also: events as a gig-poster list, event detail, reading group, subscription lengths and formats, themed subscriptions, questionnaire, gift subscription, signed first editions club, signed shelf, shipping times, Christmas cut-off and free wrapping bar, visit with hours, sell-us-your-books, booksellers, children's corner, shop sections, Friday email.
- Core-block limits: no questionnaire form (needs a forms plugin), no spines-out list view toggle. Events are posts in an Events category with an "Event" template.
