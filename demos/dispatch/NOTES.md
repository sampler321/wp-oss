# Dispatch

- Direction (owner's brief: "cutting-edge news, looking sharper"): a live wire. "Readout", one reporter in Brussels covering data brokers and ad tech. A huge condensed nameplate, the lead story beside a timestamped wire, hairline and heavy rules instead of boxes, no radius anywhere, one acid-lime marker for members-only and progress.
- Fonts: Mona Sans (variable width; condensed 850 to 900 for headlines, the nameplate and big figures, normal width for labels) and Newsreader for the reading column. Two families, no mono. This replaces the researched IBM Plex Serif because the brief asked for sharper; claimed in fonts-claim.txt.
- Palette: paper white, near-black ink, signal red for links and section labels, acid lime (with black text) for the members tag, the goals bar and the sign-up box. Variations: Wire blue, Telex (yellow paper), Night desk.
- Signature: the membership tiers with monthly and yearly prices and concrete perks, and the members-only break inside stories. Public subscriber goals with a progress bar and ruled milestone rows.
- Content model: posts in Investigations, Briefings, Explainers and Notes. Tag a story "Members" and give it the "Members-only story" template; stories put the members-only break where the free preview ends (a membership plugin hides the rest).
- Stories end with a dated corrections line; there is a corrections page, a document archive and a "how we report" page with methodology, key findings, a document shown flat and series navigation.
- No author block: the byline is written into the dateline paragraph, because the demo user has no display name.
