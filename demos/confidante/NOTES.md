# Confidante

- Direction: a women-led conversation podcast ("Say More", two friends in Levenshulme) set like a warm, loud magazine spread. The biggest thing on the home page is one sentence a host said, full width on tomato red.
- Fonts: Yeseva One (the 047 registry face, at up to 8rem for pulled quotes and titles) and Figtree for everything else. Two families, no mono.
- Palette: blush ground, oxblood text, tomato red for Ama, bottle green for Roisin, deep blush and paper for boxes. Each host keeps her colour in bios and in the transcript (speaker paragraph styles).
- Signature: the "Overheard" quote hero, arched host portraits, the two-colour transcript, the "Dear Say More" listener-letter band and a letter-episode template in green.
- Episode page: audio block, content note, chapters (timestamps link to the audio file with a #t= fragment, which starts playback there in the browser), show notes, guest card, phrase of the week, sponsor read, transcript in a details block; a sidebar with hosts, apps and support.
- Variations: Late night (oxblood), Mint tea, Talk (white, black, red).
- Core-block limits: chapters can't seek an on-page player without JS, so they open the audio file at that time instead. Episode numbers live in the post title ("63. Asking for more money").
- Demo audio is public-domain stand-in audio (White House "As Told By" series, Wikimedia Commons); captions say so.
