== Description ==

A plain, large-print theme for local community FM stations with talk, news bulletins, a community noticeboard and volunteer presenters.

Wavelength is for licensed local community FM stations: talk, hourly news bulletins, a noticeboard read out on air, and volunteer presenters. It is written and laid out for a mixed audience, much of it older: 19px text, underlined links, plain answers first, and a "Large print" style variation that makes everything bigger again.

An FM tuning dial runs across the top of every page with the needle on the station's frequency. The front page answers "what is on now" and "how do I listen" before anything else, then shows today's programmes as a timetable next to the community noticeboard. Posts are local news stories and "hear again" programmes. Patterns cover the week's timetable by day, listening help, volunteer roles and open evenings, presenters, Town Hall Talk, how the station is paid for, complaints and corrections, a transmitter work notice and a call for local bands.

Every colour, font, size and spacing value lives in theme.json, so the whole look can be changed in Appearance > Editor > Styles. The theme uses only core WordPress blocks.

Demo content: https://github.com/sampler321/wp-oss/tree/main/demos/wavelength
