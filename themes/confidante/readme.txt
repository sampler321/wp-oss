== Description ==

A podcast theme for conversation shows with two or three hosts, with episode pages that carry the player, chapters, show notes and a two-colour transcript.

Confidante is built for a two-host conversation show. Episodes are ordinary posts in topic categories (Work, Bodies, Money, Friendship, Letters). Each episode page holds the audio block, a content note, a chapter list whose timestamps open the audio at that point, show notes, a guest card and a transcript in a fold, with each host's lines in her own colour.

To add an episode, create a post, pick its topic category and insert the "Episode: full layout" pattern. Replace the audio file, then edit the chapters, notes and transcript. Change the big quote on the home page by editing the "Overheard" pattern in Appearance > Editor.

Included pages: hosts, subscribe (with a private ad-free feed note), support and advertising, write to us, episode index and transcripts. A "Recording break" template part holds a hiatus banner you can drop into the header.

The demo uses public-domain stand-in audio from the White House "As Told By" series on Wikimedia Commons. Replace it with your own files.

Every colour, font, size and spacing value lives in theme.json, so the whole look can be changed in Appearance > Editor > Styles. The theme uses only core WordPress blocks.

Demo content: https://github.com/sampler321/wp-oss/tree/main/demos/confidante
