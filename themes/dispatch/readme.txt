== Description ==

A news theme for independent reporters who run a paid publication on their own domain, with a timestamped wire, membership tiers, a members-only break and public subscriber goals.

Dispatch is a news theme for one reporter, or a small team, running a paid publication on their own domain. The home page opens with the lead story beside a timestamped wire of everything else, then public subscriber goals, recent investigations, the membership tiers and one sign-up box.

Stories are posts in categories (Investigations, Briefings, Explainers, Notes). Tag a story "Members" and give it the "Members-only story" template: the tag shows as an acid highlight in lists, and the template ends with a members-only break instead of the free sign-up box. Put the "Members-only break" pattern where the free preview should stop; a membership plugin such as Jetpack Paid Content hides what follows.

Patterns include membership tiers with monthly and yearly prices, what members get, a dated corrections line, byline and dateline, subscriber goals with a progress bar, group and institutional subscriptions, gift a year, the members' podcast feed, archive by year and a safe way to send tips.

The typefaces are Mona Sans, used condensed for headlines and the nameplate, and Newsreader for the reading column.

Every colour, font, size and spacing value lives in theme.json, so the whole look can be changed in Appearance > Editor > Styles. The theme uses only core WordPress blocks.

Demo content: https://github.com/sampler321/wp-oss/tree/main/demos/dispatch
